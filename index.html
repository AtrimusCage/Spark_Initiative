About Us

Spark Initiative

Spark Initiative is a legally registered women-led organization championing the rights and empowerment of women and girls, while advancing inclusive governance, peace and social justice, climate resilience and sustainable development for youth and marginalized communities.

Spark Initiative achieves these through community empowerment, advocacy, civic participation, social accountability, peacebuilding, capacity building, strategic partnerships, evidence-based action and support for climate-resilient and sustainable livelihoods.

🌟 Vision

An inclusive, just, and peaceful society where every voice thrives.

🎯 Mission

To empower women and girls, youth and marginalized communities to claim their rights, influence governance, and build inclusive, peaceful and resilient communities.

📈 Overall Strategic Objective

To strengthen the capacity of women and girls, youth and marginalized communities to claim their rights, influence governance and build inclusive, resilient, and peaceful communities.

Core Pillars

Spark Initiative Pillars

Our strategic pillars address interconnected rights, governance, peace, and climate resilience challenges facing communities.

Pillar 1

Women & Girls' Rights and Empowerment

To advance the rights, dignity, safety, leadership and empowerment of women and girls, particularly those experiencing discrimination, violence and exclusion.

🛡️ Rights & Safety Grassroots Action
Pillar 2

Inclusive Governance & Civic Participation

To strengthen the meaningful participation of women, youth and marginalized communities in governance and decision-making while promoting transparency, accountability and responsive leadership.

🗳️ Civic Voice Accountability
Pillar 3

Peace, Social Justice & Human Rights

To promote peaceful, inclusive and just communities by advancing human rights, conflict prevention, access to justice and social cohesion.

🕊️ Conflict Prevention Social Justice
Pillar 4

Climate Resilience & Sustainable Livelihoods

To strengthen climate resilience and sustainable livelihoods among women, youth and marginalized communities.
